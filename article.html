Stunning Solar Activity Recorded

Published: January 7th, 2011

By: NEVAanSa Public Affairs Desk

Just like the regular cycle us humans and other creatures undergo, we always end at a certain point in our lives, that we all know as death. Not only do organisms go through this in their final stages, anything you can think of has a form of death.

After death, organisms always leave something behind, whether it be mentally or physically. Stars do this physically after all of it gives up. This is called a planetary nebulae, though currently, what we have recently discovered here at NevastraiAnsa (abbreviated from now on in this article as NEVAanSa) is more than just a stellar remnant.

We have found yet another stunning visual of a NEBULA, or another term for it, NEBULAE. The recently discovered anomaly is shown to be in a very odd shape and form, reminiscent of the Mandelbrot Set, specifically in its first state. Though it is not completely verified whether this anomaly is what we have perceived it to be.

Here at NEVAanSa, we rely on statistics our machines have finalized and analyzed thoroughly, and with the technology we currently have, these work together and are programmed and built to run smoothly. The strongest match for this anomaly has been determined to be a nebula.

This nebula is currently 11111 light years away from us.
